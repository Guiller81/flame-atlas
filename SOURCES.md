The sources in the app's source list. The IDs are the ones you see in the app and in the curated files, for example `[BAS-01 p.84]`: source BAS-01, page 84 of the PDF.

No source PDF is stored in this repository: each row links to where the document is published. Each document stays under its own terms. The "Reuse note" column is what we recorded from the publishing site (the NTRS copyright field, or the licence the publisher states); check the source before reusing it.
